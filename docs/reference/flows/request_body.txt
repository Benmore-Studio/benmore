A flow binds a JSON body of at most 1 MiB (1048576 bytes) into `params`, and a `run: parse` step with `source: body` reads at most the same; a form body (and any flow or auto-CRUD body) is capped by the app's MAX_REQUEST_BODY_BYTES (default 10 MiB). A larger body answers 413 `{"error": "request body too large: the limit is ... (N bytes)", "limit_bytes": N}` and no step runs. Send larger content as an upload (api({at:'uploads'})) or in several requests.
