A flow route that collides with a route the framework registers for the app is refused at write time and by `benmore check`: auto-CRUD and its per-table sub-routes (POST /api/<table>/{id}/share and GET .../permissions on every table, .../versions and .../revert on versioned tables, .../lock, .../transition on workflow tables) and framework paths such as /health, /admin or /llms-full.txt. The refusal names the framework route, the api(at:) topic of the built-in it duplicates and a free path (e.g. POST /api/notes/:id/share-custom). The check covers every route the framework registers for that app from its source (schema, app.yaml, workflows, pages); `benmore check` also reports two flow files on one route. A colliding flow already deployed does not stop the app from loading: framework routes register first, the flow is skipped and the log names the route it hit, the flow's file, and what to do (a collision with another flow names that flow: `give this flow (flows/b.yaml) or flow a a different method or path`). A schema, app.yaml or workflows.yaml change that adds a framework route under an existing flow is reported on that flow's file. POST /api/<table>/{id}/restore stays available to a flow: the framework yields it. A flow path under a prefix the framework keeps for itself is refused the same way: /api/_, /api/v1/, /_internal/, /_admin, the sign-in paths (/login, /signup, /logout, /forgot-password, /reset-password, /verify-email, /verify-otp, /verify-mfa, /settings), /static/, /uploads/, /health, /sse/, /ws, /pdf/, /platform/, /.well-known/ and the generated files (/sitemap.xml, /robots.txt, /llms.txt, /feed.xml, /openapi.json, /manifest.json, /sw.js, /favicon.ico).
