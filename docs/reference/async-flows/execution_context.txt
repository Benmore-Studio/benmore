Queued authenticated flows rebuild a live actor after checking account, tenant, role and permissions, retain the queued credential scopes, roles, impersonation and acting-as restrictions, and carry that actor into real-time steps so tenant broadcasts work. Later grants cannot widen a queued credential. A `run: enqueue` step's `run_at` must resolve to an RFC3339 timestamp; a malformed or unresolved value fails the step before a job is inserted.
