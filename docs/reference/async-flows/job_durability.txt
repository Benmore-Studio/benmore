A finished job's closing status write (completed, failed, retry backoff) is retried, under its live lease, when the database refuses it, instead of leaving the row running for crash recovery to fail a success or re-deliver it. A running job with no lease is recovered once it started over an hour ago, which releases its project group and unique key. A webhook delivery refused by an open circuit breaker keeps its attempt and waits for the cooldown (for up to a day). `run: enqueue` with a unique_key held by an active job returns that job's id and status token, never id 0. Admin retry of a failed job whose unique_key another active job holds answers 409. A cron minute is unique in every job status, and _benmore_cron_state.last_fire only moves forward.
