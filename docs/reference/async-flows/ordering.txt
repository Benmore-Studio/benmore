Claims atomically preserve ordering for jobs whose payload carries the same non-null top-level `project_id`. A running job holds its project across hot reload until it finishes or the lease-recovery policy transitions it; lease expiry alone does not authorize a successor. Earlier retry-backoff jobs retain their (run_at, id) position. Jobs without project_id and jobs for other projects remain eligible. This does not replay completed external effects or change idempotency requirements. A group intentionally waits behind its live long-running job; inspect that job before retrying or cancelling it.
