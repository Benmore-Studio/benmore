The job runs as the signed-in caller who queued it: user_id, user_email, user_role, `user`, user_group_id and _group_id are captured from the session at enqueue (request input can't set them). A job queued by a `run: enqueue` step has no caller: it carries only the step's payload, and a payload key cannot use one of those names (api(at:"flows") → enqueue). An auth-required or role-gated async flow re-checks that account when the job starts: if it has been deactivated or deleted, must replace its password, is unverified under require_verified, (role-gated flows) no longer holds the role in the tenant it queued from, (flows with `permissions:`) no longer holds those permissions through its roles, or a non-admin is no longer in the tenant it queued from under the rules a request uses (live membership, or the tenant its email resolves to), the job fails at once with that reason and runs no step.
