A synchronous flow has a time limit: 30 s by default, at most 95 s on the hosted platform (`on.request.timeout`, api(at:"flows") → time_limits). Any flow that fans out to slow upstream APIs (carrier integrations, multi-API reports, image processing) reliably hits this. Async dispatch is the framework-native way out - no app-level polling glue, no client-side timeout handling, no AbortController dance.
