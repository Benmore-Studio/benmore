`benmore describe <app> jobs [--status pending|running|completed|failed] [--limit N] [--env dev|prod]` (MCP describe_jobs) lists the instance's job queue, newest first: `jobs` (id, job_type, status, payload, error, attempts, run_at, created_at; payload and error redacted, see error_redaction), `count` and `summary` (the number of jobs in each status). `--status` keeps one status; `--limit` defaults to 50 and is capped at 500.
