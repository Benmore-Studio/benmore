Steps that write the HTTP response (`respond:`, `redirect:`) silently no-op in the async path - by the time the worker runs, the HTTP response was already sent. Put your success/failure signal in the data layer (audit_log, ingestion_runs row, notification) and have the client read that on completion. Crash recovery requeues webhook-subscription deliveries and cron jobs with `max_attempts` > 1 and retry budget left; flow and hook jobs whose worker died mid-run are marked failed instead of risking duplicate side effects, except jobs of flows whose file declares top-level `idempotent: true` (and job types the operator lists in BENMORE_JOB_RERUN_SAFE_TYPES). Every claim counts as an attempt, so a job that kills the worker each time is failed instead of being requeued forever: an idempotent flow's job after 3 claims (or its max_attempts if higher), a webhook delivery or a retrying cron at its max_attempts. A hot reload lets the old worker finish its job with the lease still extended; a restart of the app process gives the job in progress up to 8 seconds to finish before the database closes; one still running then is recovered like a crash.
