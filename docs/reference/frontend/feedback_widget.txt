features.testing (drafts) or features.feedback (published apps) injects a feedback widget that posts to POST /api/_feedback; visitors with no account can submit. Limits: a request is read up to 3 MiB; the comment is refused past 10,000 bytes and the screenshot past 2 MiB; name, email, page, host and element selector are cut to 200/254/2048/253/1024 bytes. A visitor with no session gets 10 submissions per 10 minutes per address (429 with Retry-After). The table accepts submissions while it holds fewer than 5,000 open items (429 `the feedback inbox is full` past that; items you mark resolved, in_progress or wont_fix, or delete, do not count), and stored screenshots share 128 MiB: past it a submission is kept without its screenshot and the response says so. The widget shows a refusal's reason instead of failing silently.
