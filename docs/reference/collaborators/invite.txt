Owner: MCP invite_collaborator(app, email, role:'editor'|'viewer') or the dashboard Collaborators panel. The invite is stored pending and emailed; one address gets at most 3 invite emails per hour across the platform (later invites are still recorded and appear in the invitee's dashboard). Re-inviting an accepted collaborator only changes their role. Each new person needs a free collaborator seat on the app owner's plan (see `plans`); without one the invite is refused and nothing is stored.
