Invitee, signed in with the invited address and with that email VERIFIED: MCP collaborator_invites(action:'list') shows pending invites (app subdomain, name, role, inviter); collaborator_invites(action:'accept'|'decline', app:'<subdomain from list>') answers one. Dashboard: the banner on benmore.ai, backed by GET /platform/my-invites and POST /platform/my-invites {app, action:'accept'|'decline'} (CSRF). After accepting, the app appears in list_apps and app tools work at the invited role. An invite belongs to the account it was bound to when sent, or, while still unbound, to the verified account whose stored address is exactly the invited one. Where two accounts differ only in the case of their address, each lists and answers only its own invites.
