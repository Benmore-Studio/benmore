Paid features and the owner's plan. Custom domains (Pro and up), a dedicated SMS number and managed DNS zones (any paid plan) are checked against the app owner's plan when they are added. Collaborators take SEATS on the owner's plan: one seat per distinct person (email address) across ALL apps the owner owns, whatever the role, pending invites included; the owner, and a previous owner a transfer kept on as editor, take none. Scale (and the operator's own plans) has unlimited seats, Pro buys them as an add-on ($10/month each, on the dashboard's /upgrade page), free has none. An invite that needs a seat the owner does not have is refused with the price of a seat (invite_collaborator returns the error; the dashboard answers 402) and nothing is stored. Inviting someone who already collaborates on another app of the same owner, re-inviting, and changing a role never need a new seat. An owner over its seats (a cancelled add-on, a downgrade from Scale) keeps every collaborator; only inviting someone new is refused. When the owner switches from Pro to Scale (on the dashboard's /upgrade page, where an account that has a subscription changes plan) a seat add-on is no longer needed: it is taken off the subscription in the same change, and Stripe credits the unused part. Switching back to Pro adds no seats, and is refused while the account uses more than Pro covers with its add-ons (published apps, apps in total, collaborators, media storage): the refusal names what to free first. A transfer does not carry paid features onto a plan that does not cover them: accept_transfer, `benmore transfer-ownership <app> --accept` and the dashboard answer 409 naming each one (`this app has 2 custom domains and 1 collaborator, which your plan (free) does not include`) followed by the way out and its price; nothing moves and the transfer stays pending, so the same accept goes through once the recipient upgrades, adds the add-on, or the current owner removes those features. Initiating a transfer returns the same list as `plan_gaps` ([{kind: custom_domains|collaborators|sms_number|dns_zones|live_app, count, detail}]) with a `warning`, so the sender knows before the recipient tries. `collaborators` counts the app's accepted collaborators the recipient has no seat for (not pending invites, not the recipient's own row, not a previous owner an earlier transfer kept on as editor, not people who already hold one of the recipient's seats). `live_app` means the app is published and the recipient has no free live-app slot (api(at:"environments") `publishing`); the recipient frees one by taking an app of its own out of production (api(at:"environments") `remove_production`), or the current owner takes this app out of production before the transfer. A downgrade removes nothing: the app keeps what it has, adding more is refused by the usual gates, and for the owner GET /platform/app-branches lists what the plan no longer covers as `over_plan` (same shape; [] when the plan covers everything).
