An app's dev and prod instances share one lifecycle lock. Create/provision, archive deployment, delete, promote, refresh, reset, explicit restart, hosted reload and ownership acceptance cannot overlap; a competing operation returns a busy error. Different apps remain independent. A queued reload waits while the app is busy. Deleting an app cancels its pending reloads; a reload already running makes the delete answer busy. A restore holds the lock from preparation until the restored app is verified.
