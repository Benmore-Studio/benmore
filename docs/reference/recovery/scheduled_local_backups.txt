app.yaml `backup: {interval: 10m, keep: 3}` (interval at least 1m; keep defaults to 20) makes the app write .benmore/backups/scheduled-<UTC timestamp>.db (a VACUUM INTO copy) and keep the newest `keep` of them; auto_recovery and `benmore restore` use them like pre-migrate snapshots. These are local files on the app's disk, separate from the platform's hourly S3 snapshots (scheduled_snapshots). The schedule survives pushes and restarts. The next backup is due one interval after the timestamp in the newest scheduled-* backup's name, at once if that has already passed, or, with none yet, one interval after the app first started (a hot reload keeps that start). A name dated in the future (a clock that moved back) does not count. After an attempt, failed or not, the next comes one interval later. Each start logs `backup: scheduled every <interval> (keep <n>), next in <wait>` (see `benmore logs <app>`).
