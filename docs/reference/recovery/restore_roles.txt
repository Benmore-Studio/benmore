A database restore (restore_db, benmore restore, boot auto-recovery) rolls data back and keeps the live sign-in state (api(at:"recovery.database_restore")); in the same transaction it applies the live role state to the restored copy. A restore does not bring back a role that was taken away: afterwards no account holds a role it does not hold in the live database. For an account in both, the primary role (`_benmore_users.role`) is the lower of the snapshot's and the live one: `admin` ranks above every role and `user` (the role of a new account) below every role, so an admin demoted since the snapshot stays demoted and a role given since the snapshot is not kept; between two other role names the live one stands. A role grant (`_benmore_user_roles`, global or for one group) survives only when the live database still holds the same grant (same account, role and group) and it has not expired there, and it takes the earlier of the two expiry times; a grant made since the snapshot is not added. An account that is only in the snapshot comes back with role `user` and no role grants when the live database once held it (deleted since): its id is at or below the highest account id the live database has handed out, or a live `_benmore_audit_log` row is about it (table_name `_benmore_users`, row_id the account id; a deletion is such a row). An erased account keeps its row and counts as an account in both. An account only in the snapshot that the live database never held keeps the snapshot's primary role and role grants: restoring a backup onto a recreated or new database brings every role assignment back, except for backup accounts whose ids the new database has already handed out to accounts of its own (create no accounts before such a restore, or give those roles again). The app is never left without an admin who can sign in (an account that is neither deactivated nor held at a temporary password and is admin by its primary role or by a global admin grant with no expiry): when these rules would leave none, the accounts that are such admins now keep that standing (`admins_kept: "live"`), and when none of them exists in the snapshot, the snapshot's own admin roles and global admin grants are kept, including ones removed since (`admins_kept: "snapshot"`). Either case puts a `warning` in the answer: review who is an admin after such a restore. recovery.credentials.roles reports `primary_lowered`, `primary_reset` (accounts deleted since), `roles_kept_unknown` (accounts the live database never held, with a `note` saying so), `grants_kept`, `grants_removed`, `grants_expiry_shortened` and `usable_admins`, plus `admins_kept`, `admins_kept_count` and `warning` when admin standing was kept; the `restore_credentials` audit row carries the same. Roles an app keeps in its own tables (a membership table's role column) and record-level permissions (`_benmore_permissions`) roll back with the data. When the live database is missing or unreadable, nothing says which roles were taken away: roles stay as in the snapshot and `skipped` says why (`live_unreadable` or `no_live_database`). The audit rows of role events recorded since the snapshot are copied too: api(at:"recovery.restore_audit_rows").
