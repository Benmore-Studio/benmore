A `benmore push` or `benmore deploy` whose files the app rejects in its hot reload prints the reload error and exits non-zero (push: 1, or 2 from the save hook; deploy: 1). A rejected reload does not advance /health's reload_generation and the app keeps answering healthy on its previous configuration, while the same source would fail at the next restart. After waiting up to 25 seconds for the reload, the CLI reads the app's own record of its last reload (check_app's `app_load` error, the text `benmore check` shows) to tell a rejected reload from a slow one; a slow one keeps the warning and exit 0. The pushed files stay saved: fix the source and push again. The wait covers pushes of files that change the generated types (schema.prisma, app.yaml, flows, hooks.yaml, workflows.yaml, i18n) and every deploy.
