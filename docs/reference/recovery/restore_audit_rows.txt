A database restore (restore_db, benmore restore, boot auto-recovery) keeps the live sign-in state, and the record of how it got that way is kept with it: in the transaction that carries the credentials into the restored copy (api(at:"recovery.database_restore")), the `_benmore_audit_log` rows of account, role and credential events recorded since the snapshot are copied from the live database, each exactly once (a row counts as recorded since the snapshot when its time is at or after the newest audit time the snapshot holds and the snapshot does not already hold it; restoring the same snapshot again copies nothing twice). Copied are the rows whose table_name is `_benmore_users`, `_benmore_user_roles` or a sign-in credential table (`_benmore_sessions`, `_benmore_api_tokens`, `_benmore_passkeys`, the connector grant tables and the other credential tables): `required_password_change`, `password_change`, `password_reset`, the unverified-account claims, `role_changed` and the `role_*` grant events, the `passkey_*` events, the second-factor events (`mfa_enrolled`, `mfa_removed`, `mfa_backup_codes_generated`, `mfa_backup_code_used`), `email_change_requested` and `email_changed`, `api_token_created` and `api_token_revoked`, `session_revoked`, `account_purge`, `impersonate`, the act-as and platform-tooling account and session events, an admin's edit of an account row in /_admin (deactivation, verification, identifiers), and the `restore_credentials` rows of earlier restores; api(at:"audit.account_events") says which account events are recorded at all. A copied row keeps its time, actor and values. It keeps its id when the restored database has never handed that id out and takes the next id otherwise, so copied rows always come after the snapshot's own rows in id order. Its `restore_carried_at` column holds the time of the restore that first copied it; the column is NULL on every row written in place and is returned by GET /api/_audit. Rows about an account created after the snapshot are copied as history although the account itself is gone; the restored database can hand that user id out again, so check `restore_carried_at` and user_email before attributing such a row to a current account. A copied role event records what happened on the replaced database, and the restored roles agree with every event that took a role away: a role or grant removed since the snapshot stays removed. A role or grant given since the snapshot is not kept, although its event is copied (api(at:"recovery.restore_roles")). Audit rows of app tables recorded since the snapshot are not copied, like the data they describe. Under `compliance: hipaa` the copy only adds rows, and the append-only rules hold before and after. recovery.credentials.audit_rows gives `carried` and, when there was nothing to read, `skipped` (`live_unreadable`, `no_live_database` or `no_live_audit_log`), `live_error` when only the live audit log could not be read, or `error` when the restored database refused a copied row (a trigger an app's migration put on `_benmore_audit_log`): in both cases no audit row is copied and the restore still completes, and the replaced database kept under .benmore/backups still holds the rows. The `restore_credentials` audit row carries the same.
