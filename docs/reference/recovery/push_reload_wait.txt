`benmore push` and `benmore deploy` wait for the app's reload after any file that changes what the app loads: schema.prisma, schema.sql, schema.yaml, app.yaml, flows.yaml and flows/*.yaml, workflows.yaml, hooks.yaml, i18n/*.yaml, cron.yaml, encrypted.yaml, mcp.yaml, validators.yaml, computed.yaml, admin.yaml, retention.yaml, migrations/*.sql (not *.failed.sql), top-level *.html pages and partials/*.html. Static files do not wait. The wait is up to 25 s and adds the debounce and the reload to such a push, the save hook's included. When no newer runtime is observed in that time, the push checks the app's health a few more times: an app that answered just before the write and no longer answers at all fails the push (exit 1, or 2 from the save hook) and `benmore deploy` (exit 1), naming `benmore logs`; the files stay saved. An app that answers but has not published the reload yet gets a warning and exit 0.
