A hosted app opens at most 10 SQLite connections, keep 2 idle for 2 minutes, with a 16 MB page cache each; self-hosted defaults stay 25/10/64 MB. `BENMORE_DB_MAX_OPEN`, `BENMORE_DB_MAX_IDLE`, `BENMORE_DB_IDLE_TIMEOUT` and `BENMORE_DB_CACHE_KIB` override both. Request-log rows are written in batches by one writer per app (up to 1s late; dropped and counted when the buffer is full).
