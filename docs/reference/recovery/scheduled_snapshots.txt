On the hosted platform every production app gets a consistent database snapshot each backup round, even if data.db's modification time is unchanged. WAL-only commits and idle apps are included. Failed uploads do not count as successes and are retried next round; unavailable databases are counted separately. Dev instances are excluded. A round starts five minutes after the platform starts, then one hour after each completed round, so on a large fleet the interval can be longer than an hour. One app's backup is bounded at 21 minutes as a whole (snapshot, compression and upload).
