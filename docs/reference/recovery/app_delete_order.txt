On the hosted platform delete_app removes the app and releases everything it claimed together (custom domains, sending domains, DNS zones, Stripe Connect connections and keys, the SMS number, recordings); email/SMS pause, breaker events and send counts stay with the subdomain's sender address. If the delete fails part-way the instances are already stopped and removed but the app and its claims are kept, with nothing released; re-run the delete to finish. An ownership transfer deauthorizes its Stripe connection the same way.
