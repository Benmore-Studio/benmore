Real-time video / voice / screen-share. Two SDK surfaces. `bm.broadcast.{publish,subscribe,stats}` routes media through Cloudflare's Realtime SFU - publisher uploads ONCE, scales to hundreds of viewers with sub-second latency. The right primitive for any livestream or 1:N broadcast. `bm.webrtc.iceServers()` returns STUN + TURN config for hand-rolled peer-mesh (≤4 participants, chat-room style - use `bm.room()` for signaling). **Picking peer-mesh for a livestream is the most common reason a 'working' stream breaks at 5 viewers** - the publisher's uplink saturates uploading N copies. Use the SFU.
