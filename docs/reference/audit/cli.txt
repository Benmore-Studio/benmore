`benmore audit <app> [--table T] [--limit N] [--env dev|prod]` (MCP get_audit_log) answers the instance's audit trail, newest first: `entries` (id, action, table, row_id, user_email, created_at), `count` and `format`. `--table` keeps one table's entries; `--limit` defaults to 100 and is capped at 200. The CLI reads the concise form, without old and new values; get_audit_log with `response_format: "detailed"` returns them (editor access). A viewer sees end users by id, with emails redacted.
