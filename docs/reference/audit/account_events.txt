Which account events reach _benmore_audit_log, by `action` (table_name is `_benmore_users` unless named). Recorded: `password_change` (an account changed its own), `required_password_change` (it replaced a temporary one), `password_reset` (a reset link was used), `email_claim_unverified` and `oauth_claim_unverified` (an unverified account was claimed through its verification link or a social sign-in), `passkey_added`, `passkey_removed` and `passkeys_removed_all` (_benmore_passkeys), `role_changed` (an admin changed an account's role in /_admin), `role_granted`, `role_expiry_updated`, `role_revoked` and `role_expired` (_benmore_user_roles), `impersonate` (an admin started a support session; row_id is the account entered), `act_as_start` and `act_as_end` (_benmore_sessions), `account_purge` (account erasure) and `restore_credentials` (a database restore, which also copies the rows of these events recorded since its snapshot: api(at:"recovery.restore_audit_rows")). On a hosted app, what platform tooling does to accounts is recorded with the platform account as user_email: `superuser_created`, `app_user_created`, `app_user_deactivated` and `app_user_reactivated` (`benmore users <app> deactivate` / `reactivate`; values carry the email and `deactivated_at` before and after), `superuser_promoted`, `superuser_password_reset`, and `data_revoke_session`, `data_delegated_session` and `data_delegated_session_end` for sessions (control_plane).

Not recorded: a sign-in, successful or failed, by password, emailed code, social sign-in or passkey; a lockout; a sign-out; a sign-up; a second-factor check, passed or failed; MFA enrollment and removal; minting or revoking an API token (POST and DELETE /api/_auth/api-tokens); ending a session (DELETE /api/_auth/sessions/{id}); a profile edit and an email change. `compliance: hipaa` adds none of these. The account row carries the last sign-in time (`last_login_at`). An app that must keep a trail of sign-ups and sign-ins records one itself: hooks.yaml hooks on `_benmore_users` fire from the sign-in endpoints (insert on sign-up, update on sign-in and account changes, api(at:"hooks.triggers")), and a hook's `sql:` may INSERT INTO _benmore_audit_log or a table of the app (flow_writes).
