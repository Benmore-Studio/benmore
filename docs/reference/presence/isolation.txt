In an app with `groups:` each tenant has its own presence lists: the same slug in two tenants names two lists, keyed by the caller's effective tenant (an admin stepped into a tenant sees that tenant's lists). A slug is 1-200 characters and may not contain NUL (400). A slug matching a `ws_rooms:` pattern is a membership room, as for bm.room and bm.broadcast: a non-member's join, heartbeat and list get 403 {error:'not a member of this room'}; leave is always allowed; admins bypass. See api({at:'websocket'}) → room_authorization.
