An entry is a mapping with any of the keys `table`, `parent`, `user_field` and `members`; any other key is not read, and a write of app.yaml and `benmore check` warn about it. Write `<parent>: {}` to take every default. An entry that is not a mapping (`channels: true`, a list, or `channels:` left empty) is skipped, so nobody is enrolled: the write gate and `benmore check` refuse it. When the app starts the journal shows `auto_memberships: <parent> → <table> (auto-enrol active users on insert)`. The after-insert job enrolls users matching the configured filter; completion may follow the parent response. Verify the membership table through its guarded API. Once the job runs, its SQL target is recognized despite the generated comment and quoted identifier, so an eligible `bm.live` subscriber can refetch the updated roster. Founder membership and custom-filter/all_users behavior are unchanged. See api(at:hooks) sql_refresh for event visibility.
