History follows the row id, but each snapshot is served only to callers who could have read it: the row scope (owner, tenant, membership, record grant) is evaluated on the SNAPSHOT's own user_id / group key, not the current row's. After an ownership transfer or tenant move, the new owner sees only versions from their own tenure - earlier versions are omitted from /versions and answer 404 on /versions/{v}, /revert/{v} and ?as_of=. Record-level grants on the row also reveal its snapshots. Admins (not stepped in) see the full history.
