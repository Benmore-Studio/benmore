Versioned encrypted fields are sealed before entering history, including snapshots taken during encryption-trigger updates. Startup/reload transactionally validate and repair existing history under the owning database key and original source table/column binding; unusable keys or ciphertext stop installation. Key rotation includes history and avoids generating extra versions. Rotation is not an edit of any row. updated_at and computed values (an aggregate's parent row included) stay as they were: `?as_of=` for an instant before the rotation still answers for rows not edited since, and an `_expected_updated_at` read before it still matches. Earlier backups can retain plaintext snapshots and need their own access and retention controls.
