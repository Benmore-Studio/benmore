`owner` and `self` in unmask_roles both mean the row's owner (the account whose `user_id` is on the row), never a role, even when the app also has a role named `owner` (global `roles:`, `groups.bootstrap.founding_role: owner`, the membership role column's default or a `role:owner` gate). Holders of such a role read the field masked unless they own the row. `benmore check` and the push write gate warn when unmask_roles lists `owner` or `self` and the app has a role of that name. To let a tenant's owners unmask, give the tenant role a distinct name (e.g. `org_owner`) and list it in unmask_roles; keep `self` for the row owner.
