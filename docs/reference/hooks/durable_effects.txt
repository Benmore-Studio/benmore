An insert or delete queues its after-hook jobs and webhook subscription deliveries inside its own transaction, as an update does (single create, delete, batch create/delete, /api/_transaction, /api/_groups/create, and the soft-delete cascade, which runs in the parent's transaction). NDJSON /ingest and bulk import queue no after-hooks or deliveries. The `when:` checks and subscription matching run while the write holds the database's write lock, so keep `when:` conditions cheap on tables written in large batches. If a job cannot be queued (disk full, database busy) the write is refused with 500 and nothing is saved. Every insert/delete hook is a job (no inline SQL), queued in this order: a cascade's children before their parent; /api/_transaction updates during the operation loop, inserts and deletes after it in operation order. Event flows (`on: event`) and realtime events run after COMMIT.
