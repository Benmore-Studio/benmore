For an app whose prod or dev app.yaml sets `compliance: hipaa`, or that the platform has recorded as hipaa (the record outlives the key, and promote refuses a dev app.yaml without the key onto such an app; api(at:"compliance")), refresh_dev with `with_data` or `data_only` (also as dry_run) is refused with `copying production data into dev is not available for apps with compliance: hipaa`, as is the case where either app.yaml cannot be read; a code-only refresh (files) and seed_dev (fresh empty database) are allowed. The session recorder is forced off everywhere for such an app and an app.yaml that sets recording.enabled or recording.production true is refused by the write gate, `benmore check` and the loader.
