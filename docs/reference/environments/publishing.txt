Plans are billed on PUBLISHED (live) apps, and the publish gate is always on. Building and previewing on the dev instance is free on every plan. An app is published when it has a production instance: it was promoted, it has no dev instance (an app from before dev/prod environments, or one the legacy archive deploy created), or its production instance already runs (an app that was live before `seed-dev` gave it a dev instance). The FIRST publish of an app (`promote`, the dashboard Publish button, or an archive deploy that starts prod) needs a free live-app slot on the app OWNER's plan, whoever runs it. free: 0 live apps (1 app in total); Pro ($39/month): 5 live apps (10 apps in total); Scale ($149/month): 25 live apps (50 apps in total). On Pro and Scale each extra live app the owner bought ($10/month each) adds one to both numbers; the operator's own staff and admin plans have no limit. Without a free slot, promote ships nothing and answers `{ok:false, upgrade_required:true, feature:'publish', upgrade_url, error, live_apps_used, live_app_limit, dev_url}` (the CLI prints `error` and `→ Upgrade: <url>` and exits 1; the dashboard publish answers 402); dev is untouched. `dry_run` still previews the migration and carries `upgrade_required`, `upgrade_url` and `upgrade_note`, so check it before promising a release. `error` names the way out: the Pro plan on free, an extra live-app slot or Scale on Pro. Relay it and hand back the dev URL as a dev URL; after the owner upgrades or adds a slot, promote again. An app that is ALREADY published is never refused for the plan: an owner over the allowance (a downgrade, a cancelled add-on, an app published before the gate was on) keeps every published app serving and can still promote code to it; only publishing ANOTHER app, and creating apps beyond the total, are refused. Nothing is unpublished automatically; a live slot is freed by taking a published app out of production (`benmore remove-production <app>`, which keeps its data; see `remove_production`), or by deleting or transferring it. The limit is on apps live at the same time, so an account can rotate which of its apps are live. A promote answered `this account's add-ons are being changed right now` (no upgrade_required) passes on retry. Add-ons are bought by the account holder on the dashboard's /upgrade page (POST /platform/billing/addons takes only their own dashboard sign-in, never a CLI or MCP token), so an agent cannot buy one. GET /platform/billing/me (dashboard session or CLI token) reports `live_apps_used`, `live_apps_included`, `extra_live_apps`, `live_app_limit`, `total_app_limit`, `seats_used`, `seats_included`, `extra_seats`, `seat_limit` (limits are null when unlimited), `addons_configured` and the prices in cents.
