`benmore status` (GET /platform/app-status) reports `prod_last_promoted` as the time of the latest promote and `prod_first_promoted` as the first publish. `prod_exists` says whether the app has a production instance: false before the first promote and after `remove-production` (the kept production data is then reported by `production: 'removed'`, and the drift fields still compare dev with the kept production code, which is what a promote publishes on).
