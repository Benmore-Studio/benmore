Outbound subscriptions deliver every matching row change in the app, across all users and tenants, so only an app admin (not a stepped-in/act-as admin) may create one (403 otherwise), and deliveries stop for a subscription whose owner is no longer an active admin. Every delivery attempt (retries and crash requeues included) re-reads its subscription: one deleted, deactivated or re-pointed after the change was queued, or whose owner lost admin, is dropped. The delivered row omits the columns auto-CRUD reads hide (secret-named text columns such as api_key or *_token, and blind-index siblings), and the queued job doesn't carry the signing secret. For per-user notifications use a flow or hook scoped to that user's rows instead.
