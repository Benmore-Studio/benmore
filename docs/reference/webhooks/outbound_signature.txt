Every delivery carries `X-Webhook-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<t>." + raw body))>`, `X-Webhook-Timestamp: <t>`, `X-Webhook-Event` and `X-Webhook-Table`, where secret is the subscription's `secret`. A receiver recomputes v1 over the exact raw body, compares in constant time and refuses a `t` more than 300 s from its clock (replay). A Benmore app receives them with `verify: benmore` plus `secret: "${{ env.NAME }}"` holding the same secret (api({at:'verify'}) → benmore_subscriptions); `verify: stripe` reads only Stripe-Signature, the header form `verify: X-Webhook-Signature` expects a bare body HMAC, and `hmac_timestamp_dot_body` a bare signature, so each of those answers 401 to these deliveries. A sender with `BENMORE_WEBHOOK_LEGACY_V0=1` adds `v0=<hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, body))>`, which binds no timestamp; `verify: benmore` never accepts v0.
