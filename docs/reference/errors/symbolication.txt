Frames under this app's /static/ resolve through already-cached TSX/TS/JSX bundles only. The ?v= must match the served tree version; a mismatch leaves the frame raw and sets extra.bundle_stale. Cold caches stay raw and never trigger a build. Mapping covers at most 10 in-app frames per report. decode_stack can resolve a warm inline-map bundle; apps with their own bundler can provide static/<bundle>.map. A 401 usually means a rejected DSN key, 429 means a quota pause, and a private host is intentionally unsupported.
