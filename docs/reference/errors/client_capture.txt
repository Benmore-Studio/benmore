bm.js posts uncaught JavaScript errors and unhandled promise rejections to the same-origin POST /api/_errors. Known bodies above 64 KiB answer 413. Reports remain in _benmore_client_errors (up to 5,000 rows, pruned every 200 inserts); stack_raw retains the submitted stack; stack holds it as sent until it is rewritten with resolved frames when a matching bundle is cached. Before a report is queued only its kept shapes remain (the last 30 allowed breadcrumbs, allowed data keys, 10 tags); a request carrying a session cookie must be same-origin, as bm.js's own posts are. External @benmore/bm frontends must proxy /api/_errors alongside /api/*; the browser never contacts Sentry.
