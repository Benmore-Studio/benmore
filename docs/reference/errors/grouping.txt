_benmore_error_groups is the local source of truth, with scrubbed samples capped at 32 KiB (8 KiB for browser reports) and retention of the newest 2,000 groups (pruned every 200 occurrences). Groups use source, kind, type, normalized message and the top in-app frame or route. New occurrences clear resolution. One Sentry report per group per five minutes includes extra.occurrences_since_last_report; the throttle survives reloads. Delivery also requires the per-app 30/minute limiter and an unmuted valid DSN. A full queue drops without blocking requests; captured/dropped/throttled/forwarded/failed/muted/rejected counters count since the app last loaded or reloaded. Dropped, throttled and muted counts produce periodic client_report envelopes when configured. Shutdown drains for at most two seconds.
