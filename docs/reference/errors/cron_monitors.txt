cron.yaml jobs accept monitor: true, monitor: false (off) or a map with checkin_margin and max_runtime integer minutes (1–1440, defaults 5 and 30). Both named-map and jobs-list shapes validate the same way; benmore check warns when SENTRY_DSN is unset. The reporter queues Sentry check_in envelopes without blocking cron execution: in_progress at start, then ok/error with the same random id and elapsed seconds after success, panic, rollback or step failure. The first successful check-in per monitor/DSN/process supplies monitor_config; unchanged config stays cached over reloads and changed schedules/margins resend it. Crontab aliases expand to five fields; every-duration jobs use interval minutes rounded to the scheduler's next minute tick. Schedules restricting both day-of-month and weekday warn and skip monitoring because Benmore intersects those fields while Sentry unions them; the job itself still runs unchanged. The timezone is the server's actual local IANA zone; unidentified zones warn and skip crontab monitoring. No DSN means no check-in; category backoff, guarded HTTPS transport and the reporter limiter still apply.
