ERROR_SLACK_WEBHOOK enables best-effort fatal/error notification at most once per group per hour, persisted across reloads. Slack has its own 30/minute budget (server and browser reports separately), never Sentry's. The URL must be a public https URL when it is set (env set, benmore check) and is checked again at delivery. Visitor text is escaped for Slack and scrubbed before sending; client IP is not sent.
