A failed local group write still exports to Sentry and Slack (without the per-group throttle; the per-minute budgets still apply) and the group table is retried on the next event. A failing cron run is reported once (cron_step/cron_panic/cron_transaction, never also job_failed). Apps loaded by benmore test or verify never deliver to Sentry or Slack; their groups stay local. The delivery test (test_error_reporting) spends its own budget and records its group resolved. Invalid get_errors filters answer bad_request and a full reporter queue busy. job, cron, hook and worker failures are exported with the same redacted text the job row and journal keep, never the raw internal error. The export scrubber also replaces each env value URL-escaped (path and query form) and the runtime-only credentials of the process environment. POST /api/_errors admits 30 reports a minute per client address (an IPv6 client by its /64) and answers 429 past that; browser reports may fill only half of the reporter queue, so a flood cannot drop server, job, cron or check-in events. bm.js marks its report X-Benmore-Background: 1 (no idle_timeout activity) and clears breadcrumbs, tags and its dedupe window on sign-in, sign-out and tenant changes. A cron run's closing check-in is sent even when the app reloads during the run.
