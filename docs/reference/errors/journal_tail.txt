`benmore tail <app> [--lines N] [--since 10m] [--grep text] [--env dev|prod]` (MCP tail_logs) returns the app's journal: FLOW ERROR and SERVER ERROR lines, panics, warnings. It reads at most 1000 journal lines (`--lines` defaults to 100 and is capped at 1000). `--grep` searches the whole 1000-line window (within `--since`) and returns the last `--lines` matches. The answer has `lines_read` (the window searched) and `matched`, and when it is cut - older lines not read, more matches than `--lines`, or `--lines` over the cap - `truncated: true` and a `hint` saying which; the CLI also prints that hint as a final stderr line. To find one failed request, grep for its request id.
