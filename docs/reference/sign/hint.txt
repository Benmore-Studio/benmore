Sign failures stop the call before anything is sent, when the step runs (the write gate does not check recipe names): the flow answers 500 `flow_no_response` naming the failed step, and the app log names the cause: a missing env var by name (`env.NAME had no value in scope`), `sign: unknown recipe "x" (known: aws_sigv4, bearer_token, hmac_sha256_body, oauth_client_credentials, stripe_webhook_hmac)`, or `cannot mix recipe: <name> with inline compute/headers`.
