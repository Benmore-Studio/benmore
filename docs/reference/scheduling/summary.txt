Per-record deferred tasks + human approvals. The framework's cron is global; this adds 'do X for THIS record at THIS time' (reminders, SLAs, dunning, scheduled flow runs) plus a lightweight approval primitive. A per-app sweeper (30s) fires due tasks: reminders and successful flow tasks post an in-app notification. Powers Reminder/ScheduleAction/Approval - see api({at:'components'}).
