GET /api/_signed_url answers `{url, path, expires_in}`; `expires_in` is the lifetime that was signed, in seconds. Its `expires_in` query parameter (what bm.signedUrl sends `ttlSeconds` as) is whole seconds (`300`) or a duration (`30s`, `90m`, `2h`), at least 1s. A duration's fraction of a second is dropped, and more than 6 hours gives 6 hours. Left out or empty, the link lives 1 hour, or 4 hours for an audio or video file (bm.signedUrl sends 300 seconds for anything that is not audio or video). A value that cannot be read or is under one second (`abc`, `0`, `-5`, `1.5`, `500ms`) answers 400 `{error, code: "invalid_expires_in", docs}` with no link: a signed link is a bearer credential and never lives longer than the lifetime asked for. A public file on the media CDN needs no signature and answers `expires_in: 0`. See api({at:'uploads'}) → signed_link_lifetime for serve_file's lifetimes.
