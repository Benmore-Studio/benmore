Per app process: at most 5000 WebSocket and 5000 SSE connections, 10 of each per signed-in non-admin user, and 30 of each per client address for anonymous clients, where an IPv6 client counts by its /64. Over a limit /ws and /sse/events answer 429 (503 at the process-wide ceiling). One WebSocket connection holds at most 256 joined rooms; a join of a new room past that gets {type:'error', room, body:'too many rooms on this connection'} (bm.room() uses one connection per room).
