HTML-stack pages and engine: raw pages initialize window.__bm_i18n = {lang, strings} before authored scripts. The initial language is seo.lang when that dictionary exists, otherwise the app's default (i18n/.default or en); the emitted lang names the selected dictionary. Apps without a nonempty selected dictionary receive no bootstrap. JSON/script escaping preserves hostile translation text as data. Server-rendered templates choose their language separately.
