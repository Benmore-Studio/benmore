New record shares resolve an existing account using the email password-login rule (trim/lowercase the request, then SQLite NOCASE lookup). Exactly one matching account stores its actual email spelling; the response email and audit shared_with use that same spelling. Multiple matching accounts, including unverified or deactivated legacy case variants, return 409 before changing a grant. A blank recipient after trimming returns 400. When no account exists, the original recipient spelling is stored unchanged for compatibility: a future account must have that exact stored spelling and satisfy the email-proof gate; future case variants are not automatically resolved. Share again after the account exists to resolve its spelling. Existing grants are not rewritten, and ACL reads remain exact, so an old mismatched higher-permission grant is not activated. Verified/grandfathered email proof, current tenant bounds, scopes, CSRF, sharing authority, expiry and field masks still apply. This does not change account provisioning or OAuth identity matching.
