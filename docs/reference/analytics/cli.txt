`benmore analytics <app> [--env dev|prod]` (MCP get_analytics) answers the instance's last 7 days: `pageviews`, `unique_visitors`, `sessions`, `bounce_rate`, `avg_duration_ms`, `pages_per_session`, `new_visitors`, `returning_visitors`, `live_visitors`, `prev_pageviews`, `prev_unique_visitors` and the lists `top_pages`, `top_referrers`, `entry_pages`, `exit_pages`, `devices`, `browsers`, `countries`, `campaigns` and `daily`. With no `--env` and no pin the CLI reads prod once the app has a prod instance, else dev.
