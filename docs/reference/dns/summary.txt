Platform-hosted DNS (managed zones, Pro). Instead of copying records to an external DNS provider, the app owner points their domain's NAMESERVERS at the platform once (dashboard → Settings → Managed DNS, or `benmore dns <app> add <domain>`); the platform then auto-creates every record its features need - custom-domain routing + ownership TXT, email DKIM CNAMEs - and records for domains attached later are added at the zone's next check (see gotchas). Delegation is detected within ~5 minutes of the registrar change; the email verifier is nudged the moment the zone activates. A record editor covers everything else (A/AAAA/CNAME/TXT/MX/SRV/CAA; NS/SOA protected).
