auth is a scalar-value map; nesting oauth under auth is invalid. Built-in providers use their environment credentials. Custom provider definitions are scoped to the current app, add new names, and do not override built-in definitions: an entry named `google`, `github` or `microsoft` is skipped whole, so none of its keys is read and the provider keeps its built-in endpoints, scopes and fields. A write of app.yaml and `benmore check` warn about such an entry and name the keys written in it. A custom provider's entry under top-level `oauth:` takes the keys `auth_url`, `token_url`, `userinfo_url`, `email_field`, `name_field`, `email_endpoint`, `scopes` and `trust_email`; any other key there is not read, and a write of app.yaml and `benmore check` warn about it. `trust_email` takes true/false, and yes/no, on/off, 1/0 mean the same; left out, it is false. Any other value is refused at write time and by `benmore check`, and an app that carries one gives the provider no trust (its addresses are not trusted). Both CLIENT_ID and CLIENT_SECRET must be present for BYO login controls and routes.
