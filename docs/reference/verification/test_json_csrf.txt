A cookie-authenticated step sends the CSRF token as a signed-in page of the app does. A `json:`, `json_array:` or `ndjson:` step, and a step with no body on any method but GET, HEAD and OPTIONS (`api: "DELETE /api/notes/1"`), sends it in `X-CSRF-Token`, as the browser SDK does. A `data:` or `files:` step sends it in the `_csrf` field of its form body, and also in the header on a method whose form body the server does not read (any but POST, PUT and PATCH). `anon: true` steps, `bearer: true` steps and the steps after a `set_auth` send none.
