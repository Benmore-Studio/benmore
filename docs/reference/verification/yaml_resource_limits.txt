YAML writes and source/complete checks refuse oversized or excessively wide mappings before running the remaining per-file validators. The limits are 8 MiB and 200 million pairwise key comparisons summed across mappings/documents (about 20,000 keys in one mapping). Reduce or split the configuration, then re-write the file. Ordinary inputs receive the syntax, credential and file-specific checks; this is a resource refusal, not permission to skip validation.
