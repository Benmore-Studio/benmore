A YAML test step `flush: jobs` drains the background job worker, `flush: retention` runs retention once, and `flush: scheduled` runs one scheduled-task sweep (the 30 s sweeper of `api(at:"scheduling")`), so a test can seed due or stale-claimed `_benmore_scheduled_tasks` rows with a `sql:` setup and assert what the sweep did. The step reports pass once the sweep returns; assert the outcome with a following `sql:` step.
