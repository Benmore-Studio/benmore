Inserts into a table the connector enrolled for approved writes keep working after reloads and restarts when the table has an encrypted field or a same-row computed field. A table with a `blind_index:` field is not enrolled: it cannot take approved writes, and enabling them fails with `blind-index trigger closure for <table>.<column> is not yet admitted` (`connector_command_sql`, 'Framework triggers'). Every load drops and creates those fields' triggers again, and each load step that creates triggers recreates an enrolled table's revision triggers after them, in the same transaction, from the same text: the incarnation, every row revision and the enrollment stay as they were, so nothing is re-approved. The revision triggers must fire before the table's other triggers (SQLite runs a table's triggers newest first). If another trigger on an enrolled table fires before the revision triggers, the connector refuses the enrollment (`revision triggers on "<table>" no longer fire before the table's other triggers`), `mcp status` shows `revision_enrollment: out_of_order` with a problem line, the serving app's check (`check_app`) reports an error naming the table, and `benmore mcp enable <app> --writes` (or a reload) puts them first. While they are out of order, an insert that another trigger rewrites (an encrypted or computed field) fails, auto-CRUD included: 400 `Insert failed: the write was rejected`, log `write rejected (insert <table>): revision ledger row missing`, commands `unavailable_provider`.
