Every lock refusal is 409 Conflict, never 423. Acquire answers {error:'record is locked by another user', locked_by, expires_at} (two acquires racing for a free row can get the error alone). PATCH, DELETE, batch update/delete, /api/_transaction and workflow transitions on a row another user has locked answer 409 {error:'record is locked by another user'} (plus locked_by in dev mode) when the caller may edit or delete that row. Optimistic concurrency (_expected_updated_at) also answers 409, as {error:'conflict', message:'Record was modified by another user'}, so tell the two apart by `error`: 'record is locked by another user' is a held lock (wait, or ask the holder), 'conflict' is a stale write (re-read the row and retry).
