A lock expires 30 s after its last acquire; there is no longer lock. To hold a row through a long edit, the holder POSTs the lock route again before it expires (every 20 s or so): the same user's acquire is idempotent and moves expires_at forward. An expired lock stops blocking at once (every check compares expires_at) and is deleted by a sweep every 10 s or replaced by the next acquire; checking a lock (PATCH, DELETE, batch, transaction, status) never writes. An anonymous PATCH on an `update: anon` table is blocked by any lock, like DELETE and batch.
