Sending SMS from an app. On the hosted platform every app can text OUT OF THE BOX once the platform's toll-free number clears carrier verification: flow/hook `run: sms` steps and framework phone-OTP auth deliver via the platform SMS service (AWS End User Messaging) from a shared toll-free number. Transactional only (OTP, account notifications), US/Canada destinations by default, tight plan-based daily quotas because every message has real cost. Explicit per-app providers (SMS_ACCOUNT_SID/Twilio, SMS_WEBHOOK_URL) always take precedence. 'Send as your brand' for SMS means a DEDICATED registered number (no DKIM analog): paid-plan apps can request one - the platform leases a toll-free number, auto-submits the carrier registration from a short company form, and switches origination automatically on approval (days to ~2 weeks).
