With app.yaml `compliance: hipaa` every upload goes to the private tier: POST /api/_upload defaults to kind=private and refuses any other kind (400), POST /api/_files stores under uploads/private/_files/, and a multipart POST /api/<table> stores its files under uploads/private/<table>/ (the stored column holds that /uploads/private/... reference; read it with bm.signedUrl or `run: serve_file`). The write gate and `benmore check` refuse a literal public kind in app source. Apps without it are not affected. See api(at:"compliance") -> private_uploads.
