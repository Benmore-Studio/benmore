Anything that names a person by email applies only to an account whose email is verified (_benmore_users.verified = 1) or grandfathered (_benmore_users.email_grandfathered = 1: an unverified account that existed before email proof was required of new accounts; accounts created since must verify. In an app that keys any grant by email, a grandfathered account that is still unverified verifies at its next password sign-in: POST /login stops with 'Please verify your email to continue' and POST /api/_auth/token returns 403 email_verification_required, and a one-hour link is emailed at most every five minutes. Sessions that already exist keep working until they end, and if the link can't be sent the sign-in continues): tenant membership when groups.user_field is an email column (tenant resolution, role_field, _switch_group, the group user directory, scheduled-flow execution), member-of: and ws_rooms: rules whose user column contains "email", record shares (_benmore_permissions grantee email), approvals addressed by email, and pending user_roles invites (activated at verification, not at signup). An account becomes verified through the auth.verify_email link, a successful emailed auth.otp login (not SMS), a confirmed email change, or a provider-verified OAuth sign-in. Until then the user signs in normally but has no email-keyed tenant, rooms or shares, and POST /api/_groups/create returns 403 email_unverified. Apps that key tenants by email need one of those proofs (auth.verify_email or auth.otp, ideally auth.require_verified); membership keyed by user id is unaffected. A provider-verified OAuth sign-in to an existing never-verified account claims it for the proven owner: its password, MFA factor, sessions and API tokens are revoked (audited as oauth_claim_unverified). A grandfathered account is marked verified by that sign-in but not claimed. The check is on the session's own account: a session whose account is unverified and not grandfathered gets no email-keyed tenant even when another account holding the same address in different case is verified (possible only in a database whose case-insensitive email index could not be created). A persistent API token (bmr_/bma_) carries the account's grandfathered standing like its sign-in sessions do.
