DELETE (single or batch) of a row in a table with deleted_at also soft-deletes its live children: rows of tables that have a foreign key on the parent's id and a deleted_at column of their own, one level deep (/api/_transaction deletes do not cascade). Each child is deleted as the caller who deleted the parent, through the ordinary delete pipeline: the child table's delete mode and RBAC scope must admit the caller, the row must be in their delete scope and not locked by someone else, and a before_delete hook may refuse it. A child the caller could not delete with DELETE /api/<child>/<id> stays live (another owner's comment on your post, another tenant's row). Each cascaded child gets an audit row (action delete) and fires on_delete hooks, on_delete flows and webhook subscriptions like any delete. When both tables carry the groups key, children in another tenant than the parent are never cascaded, for admins too. To delete every child regardless of owner, do it deliberately in an on_delete hook on the parent: `UPDATE comments SET deleted_at = datetime('now') WHERE post_id = {{id}} AND deleted_at IS NULL`.
