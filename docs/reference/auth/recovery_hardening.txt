POST /forgot-password requires CSRF like the other auth forms (the auto-injected `_csrf` field or X-CSRF-Token), answers immediately and sends the email off the request path, and a new reset request does not retire the address's earlier unused links: each link stays valid until it expires (1 hour) or any of them is used, at most 3 are live per address, and a request past that sends nothing until one ends. POST /api/_auth/token applies and feeds the same per-IP lock as /login. It issues its session only while the password checked is still current: a password reset, password change or unverified-account claim that lands while the password is being checked makes the exchange answer 401 `{"error":"credentials changed; sign in again"}`, and an `mfa_code` is spent only together with the session it buys. That lock (25 failed sign-ins in 15 minutes across /login, /api/_auth/token and passkey sign-in) counts an IPv6 client by its /64, as the rate limits do. auth.signup_fields may not name role, verified, secret-shaped or tenant-key columns: the write gate refuses them and signup ignores them at runtime. Settings > delete account purges like `run: purge_current_user` (sessions, tokens, passkeys and grants go; a non-login tombstone keeps the id), and like it refuses the app's last usable durable admin. Temporary sign-in step cookies (OTP, MFA, passkey, OAuth) are bound to their step, so finish a half-done sign-in once after upgrading if it fails. The cap on pending passkey challenges per client (20) also counts an IPv6 client by its /64, as the sign-in lock and the other per-address auth limits do.
