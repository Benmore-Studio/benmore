POST /signup has its own abuse limits, checked after the address and password pass validation and before the account is created. (1) Per client address: 30 sign-ups per 10 minutes for one address (an IPv6 client counts by its /64), per app; `auth.signup_rate_limit: "<count>/<window>"` in app.yaml changes it (e.g. "200/hour" for an app whose users share one office or classroom address; windows s, m, h, d or a Go duration; a malformed value is refused at write time). (2) Per mailbox, only on apps that mail the new address (auth.otp or auth.verify_email): at most 3 sign-ups per 15 minutes whose mail lands in one inbox. The inbox is counted with plus-addressing, letter case and Gmail's dots folded, so me+1@x.com, me+2@x.com and Me@x.com share one budget; the accounts themselves stay distinct, exactly as typed. Past either limit the form redirects back with `?error=Too many sign-up attempts. Try again later.` and a JSON client gets 429 with Retry-After; no account is created and no mail is sent. The email-change confirmation mail and the forgot-password mail count their existing 3-per-15-minutes cap per mailbox the same way. To create many accounts at once (seeding, QA), use `benmore users <app> create` or an admin flow, not the public form.
