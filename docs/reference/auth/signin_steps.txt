Half-finished MFA and emailed/texted-code sign-ins use opaque, purpose-bound, one-use challenges stored only as hashes in the app database. They are bound to the account's current email and credential generation: password change/reset (including same-hash operator reset), MFA activation/disable, deactivation and full-account access revocation invalidate old steps. Purge removes pending steps; restoring a snapshot cannot revive a consumed or revoked step. The browser password proof is rechecked when a challenge, enrollment grant or direct browser session is issued; API token password exchange is a separate path. MFA challenges last 5 minutes, OTP challenges 10 minutes; each account keeps at most 20 live steps, oldest first. Completion consumes the challenge and factor and creates the session (or next MFA/OTP step) in one transaction, so a failed save consumes no successful proof. A password reset between MFA and OTP invalidates that OTP step too. Old unbound step cookies are refused after upgrading; finish by signing in again. Existing completed sessions are unaffected. Step posts refuse another Origin or Sec-Fetch-Site cross-site/same-site before spending attempts. Native clients may omit those headers.
