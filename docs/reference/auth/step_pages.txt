The paths the sign-in handlers send a person to are pages the app serves; the framework serves only the POST handlers: /verify-mfa (authenticator code), auth.mfa_setup_redirect (default /mfa-setup; enrolling a role listed in require_mfa_for_roles), /verify-otp (auth.otp emailed or texted code), /forgot-password and /reset-password?token= (the emailed link). New HTML scaffolds ship static/verify-mfa.html, mfa-setup.html, verify-otp.html, forgot-password.html and reset-password.html, and the login page links to /forgot-password. They are plain POST forms that work without script (verify-mfa posts mfa_code and next; verify-otp code and next; forgot-password email; reset-password token, password and confirm); lib/auth.ts initAuthPage shows the ?error= and ?success= the handlers redirect back with and fills the hidden next and token fields; mfa-setup needs script (bm.mfa.enroll, bm.mfa.verify). An app scaffolded earlier serves its SPA index.html at those paths, signed out: an MFA account cannot finish signing in and a reset link opens the home page. Copy the pages from a new scaffold (`benmore new <tmp>`), or build them as above (api(at:"mfa") login_steps). At a step, /login answers a JSON request with 200 {ok: true, otp_required | mfa_required | mfa_setup_required: true, redirect} and sets the step cookie; bm.auth.signIn, signUp and passkey.signIn send the browser to redirect.
