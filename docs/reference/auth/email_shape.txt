An account email is one plain mailbox wherever an address is admitted: signup, the auth.domain / auth.allow_emails gate, OAuth sign-in, email change (request and confirmation) and `type: email` flow inputs share one rule. It needs exactly one @ with a local part and a domain, at most 254 bytes of valid UTF-8, no whitespace, control or invisible format characters (zero-width, bidi overrides) and none of , ; < > ( ) [ ] \ " : |. OAuth sign-in (BYO and managed) applies the rule to the provider's address before any account is matched or created, and returns to the login page with an error otherwise; | and invisible characters are refused outside signup too. An existing account whose stored email breaks the rule keeps its sessions and password sign-in in an ungated app, but cannot pass an auth.domain gate or sign in through OAuth until an operator corrects the address.
