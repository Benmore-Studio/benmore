Control characters in q (NUL, escape, ...) separate words exactly as a space, tab or newline does, so `rep%00` searches for `rep`. A q with no words at all (blank, or only spaces and control characters) is ignored and the list comes back unfiltered. One search takes at most 8 different words shorter than 3 characters (a repeat in any ASCII case counts once): each is a separate scan, not an index lookup. A q with more answers 400 naming the limit, on the plain list, `count=true`, `page=` and `cursor=` alike; nothing is searched with some words dropped. Words of 3+ characters are not limited.
