SUBSTRING, case-insensitive. `?q=test` matches 'test', 'Testing', AND 'contest' (the substring anywhere in any indexed column) - exactly 'rows containing this string', no client-side fuzzy layer needed. A query is split into words at whitespace, and a row matches when EVERY word is a substring of some searched column (AND; the words may sit in different columns, in any order). Words of 3+ characters use the trigram index; a 1-2 character word (`in`, `a`, `C#`, `5`) is matched with LIKE over the same columns, where `%` and `_` are literal and only ASCII letters ignore case. So `third in quarter` matches a row holding all three words. There is no phrase search: `to do` matches rows holding `to` and `do` anywhere, and quotes are literal characters. Punctuation (emails, UUIDs) is safe - tokens are phrase-quoted. Existing FTS tables auto-migrate to trigram on the next app boot (lossless rebuild from the content table).
