`benmore describe <app> table <t>` (MCP describe_table) and api(at:'table:<t>') report `access` - for each of read/write/update/delete the effective `mode`, `declared_by` (table, default or implicit) and `effect`, and for read/update/delete `rows`: what a signed-in non-admin reaches (none, admin_only, all, member_of, owner_or_role, owner, tenant, tenant_root; `tenant_bound` when the caller's tenant also limits it). Describe's behaviors follow it: owner_scoping lists only the ops whose rows are owner-only, next to membership_scoping, owner_or_role_scoping, org_scoping and broad_read, plus field_encryption (unmask roles, blind index) and hidden_columns.
